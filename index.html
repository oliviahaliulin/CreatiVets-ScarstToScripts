Where Healing Finds Its Voice

Every scar
holds a story.

Scars to Scripts transforms veterans' experiences into powerful theatrical performances that foster healing, understanding, and human connection.

Once a year, veterans are paired with professional screenwriters to uncover a story that deserves to be told. Through weekly collaboration, personal experiences become original one-act plays performed live by professional actors at the Tennessee Performing Arts Center.

This is more than a performance. It is an opportunity to witness what can happen when someone is given the space, support, and courage to tell their story.
