Where healing finds its voice

From lived experience
to the stage.

Scars to Scripts transforms veterans’ experiences into original one-act plays performed live at TPAC.

Through the CreatiVets screenwriting program, veterans are paired with professional screenwriters who help shape deeply personal experiences into stories. The process offers a creative way to revisit, reinterpret, and share what can otherwise be difficult to express.

The evening is more than a performance—it is an act of listening. Audiences witness courage, resilience, and the transformative power of art while helping future veterans take part in the program.
